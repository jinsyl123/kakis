What's in it for you.

01 You play free Your own game every week, at your court, on your night. Hosts never pay to play.
02 A cut of every night A share of each session goes to the host. Exact split set with you before your first season.
03 Never alone Every game has a host and a co-host. Miss a week and your co-host runs it.
04 Your people You end up knowing everyone. Hosts are the most connected person in the room, on purpose.

Nobody starts as a host.

The best hosts are regulars who already care. So hosting is a ladder, not a job interview.

STEP 1 Regular Come three times. You know names, you know the routine.
STEP 2 Co-host Help run a season next to a host. Cover the odd week.
STEP 3 Host Run your own game, with a co-host of your own. That co-host is the next host.

Host questions.

What if nobody shows up?

We don’t open your game until at least six people near you have signed up for that night. You won’t be standing on an empty court.

Do I need to be good at the sport?

No. You need to be warm and on time. Mixed levels are the point. The app suggests fair rotations.

How much time is it, really?

The game itself, plus ten minutes early and about fifteen minutes of admin a week, mostly tapping “send” on messages the app drafts for you.

What if I need to stop?

Hosting is one season (six weeks) at a time. When it ends, keep going, or hand the game to your co-host. Nobody’s game dies because one person got busy.

How much do hosts earn?

You play free, plus a share of every session. We’ll agree the split with you before your first season. Founding hosts help us set it.

Can I host something other than tennis?

Yes. Pickleball, badminton, run club, hikes, bouldering, a cook-along, clay night, mahjong, board games, quiz team, a volunteer crew. If it’s weekly, small, side by side and ends with food, it works.
