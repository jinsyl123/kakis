Concept screen

Crossed the line? Keep the crew.

Most crews don't want to stop. Pick a new challenge together, or turn into a weekly game with the same people.

Fair questions.

I've never done this. Is that okay?

That's the only requirement. Every crew starts at zero, so nobody is the worst for long.

Can I come alone?

Most people do. You can also sign up with a friend and join the same crew.

What if I miss a week?

You get a short catch-up from the coach, and the crew saves your seat. Six weeks is short enough to stay with it.

How much is it?

One price for the six weeks, covering the coach and the venue. Food after is pay-your-own.

What happens after the finale?

Pick another challenge with your crew, or keep meeting weekly. Either way, you keep the people.

Who are the coaches?

People who are good at the thing and patient with beginners. Every coach is vetted, and every session is in a public place.

Start at zero with us.

The first crews start in Singapore. Pick a challenge and a night, and we'll put you in a crew of ten.
