How it works.

001

Pick a table near work

Choose your area and a weekday. We seat you at a table of eight people who work nearby.

002

Eat together, weekly

Same table, same people, 45 minutes. A host keeps the seat choped and says hi to anyone new.

003

Keep coming back

Your seat is held every week, so come when you can. After a few lunches, the table usually plans a dinner.

Bring it to your office.

Companies and agencies can run cross-team lunch tables. People meet colleagues from other teams, the same ones every week, over a meal they'd eat anyway.

Ask about your office

Questions.

I only have 45 minutes. Is that enough?

That's the point. Tables run 12:30 to 1:15, a short walk from the offices around them.

How much is it?

You pay for your own lunch. Joining a table is free while we pilot.

Can I come alone?

Everyone does. The host keeps the table and introduces you on your first day.

What if I miss a week?

Your seat stays choped. Just tap "not this week" so someone from the waitlist can try it.

Is this networking?

No pitching, no business cards. It's lunch with people who happen to work nearby.

Is it safe?

Tables are in busy public food centres, hosts are vetted, and you can leave or report anything privately.
