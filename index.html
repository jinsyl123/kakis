Fair questions.

Small, cheap, drop-in friendly, phones away. That’s the whole rulebook.

How do I get into a game?

Tell us your thing, your nights and your area. We show two or three games that fit. Pick one, and it’s yours each week.

Do I have to come every week?

No. Your usual spot is held, and you tap “can’t make this one” when you’re out. A core of regulars forms on its own, and new people join any week.

Can I come alone?

Most people do. The host says hi, gets you into the first game, and introduces you around. Nobody has to break the ice from zero.

Is it safe to come alone?

Every host has an intro call and an ID check before their first game. Games happen in public places: courts, community halls, parks, hawker centres. There are women-led and women-only games, hosts can remove anyone, and you can flag anything privately.

What if I’m not very good?

Levels are mixed on purpose. The host rotates partners so games stay fair and nobody sits out all night.

How much is it?

About your share of the court, plus a small cut for the host who runs it. Food after is pay-your-own and optional (but don’t skip it).

Is this a dating app?

No. No swiping, no profiles. A game, a small group, food after.
