A step outside the group

After a few weeks together, you'll get a gentle invite to do something small with people you already know. That's usually where friendships start.

What it will never do.

The nudges only use what you choose to tell us, and you can switch them off in one tap.

  • Pick your friends for you
  • Share anything you didn't choose to share
  • Message anyone pretending to be you
  • Nudge you more than once a week
  • Sell or pass on your data to anyone

The group itself is simple.

Same people, every week

About eight of you, same night, same place. Come when you can; your spot is held.

Something to do

Badminton, mahjong, a run, a cook-along. Hands busy, conversation easy.

Food after

Supper together after every session. Optional, but it's where the good conversations happen.

Questions people ask.

Is an algorithm choosing my friends?

No. You choose a group by activity, night and area. The nudges only point out things you already have in common with people you've already met.

Isn't this a bit creepy?

It would be if it guessed things about you. It doesn't. It only uses what you type in and choose to share, and every nudge is something you can ignore.

What do I have to share?

Nothing beyond your name and contact. Hometown, interests and what you'd like to do more of are all optional, and you can change them anytime.

Can I come alone?

Most people do. The host introduces you, and you're in a small group from the first night.

Is it safe?

Hosts are vetted, groups meet in public places, and you can leave or report anything privately.
